TCZ Ground Game V2.2.1 (offline) - Level 1 Kane, PA -> Level 2 Bradford, PA
1. Double-click index.html (opens in Safari or Chrome). No internet or server needed.
   Or double-click "Open TCZ Ground Game.app" (first time: right-click > Open if macOS warns).
2. Click the map, then walk Kane's streets with Arrow keys or WASD (hold to keep walking).
   Press E next to a challenger. Seats sit at real landmarks; the gold arrow points to the current quest.
3. Clear the Void shrine (Evergreen Park) and the Chronos shrine (Historical Society) to open the gate
   at the Community Center, then beat the closer L01-M07. That earns a bus ticket: press T or use the
   Travel panel to ride to Level 2, Bradford.
4. One new seed per day. The Debug panel has the Rule 2 extras stub, travel, "ignore daily limit" and "advance day".
5. Touch / mouse: the 🎮 Pad button shows on-screen arrows + E (auto on phones). ⛶ Full = fullscreen.
6. Save is automatic (browser localStorage). Save panel: export / import / reset.
Note: progress lives in the browser you play in. Safari and Chrome keep separate saves.
